About This Assessment

This screening tool analyzes reading behavioral patterns to identify potential indicators of dyslexia. It evaluates multiple factors including reading speed, accuracy, letter reversal frequency, comprehension ability, and spelling proficiency. Results are provided with SHAP-based explanations for transparency.
